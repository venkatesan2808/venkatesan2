India is a diverse country known for its rich culture, traditions, and history. CHINA

China is one of the world’s oldest civilizations with a rich history and culture. It is famous for landmarks like the Great Wall and its unique tradition.China blends ancient traditions with rapid modern development. It is known for its culture, technology, and delicious cuisine.
